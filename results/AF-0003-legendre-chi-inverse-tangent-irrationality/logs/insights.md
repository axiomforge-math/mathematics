# Insights

The ideas that made the proof work. For each: the idea, why it works, where it is used in
the manuscript, and its limits. Scope: the ideas of this contribution only.

## More poles than rows

* **Idea.** Take K = 7h/6 poles at the negative half-integers.
* **Mechanism.** The extra poles improve the arithmetic profile more than they cost in the archimedean estimate.
* **Used in.** Sections 2 and 5.
* **Scope and limits.** Constants found numerically; margins small.

## A band of double zeros for q = 81

* **Idea.** For q = 81 put double zeros at 1, …, A on the positive axis.
* **Mechanism.** This shifts the balance between the prime-2 bound and the archimedean decay enough to reach q = 81.
* **Used in.** Section 2, Theorem 3.3.
* **Scope and limits.** Does not reach q = 79 (Section 9).

## Parity at the prime 2

* **Idea.** Separate the interpolation nodes by parity in the 2-adic expansion.
* **Mechanism.** Gives a bound −(140/99)h² − O(h log h) for the general construction.
* **Used in.** Section 3, Table of Appendix B.
* **Scope and limits.** Exact values lie above the finite bound by 3–56 at h ≤ 72.
