# 001 · A fallback range q ≥ 127

## Goal

Prove irrationality of χ₂(2/q) for odd q ≥ 127 with a first choice of pole ratio.

## Method

Hankel determinants with half-integer poles, exact rational certificates.

## Result

A candidate proof for q ≥ 127, superseded.

## Why it failed or what it taught

The pole ratio 7/6 (general construction) and the band of double zeros (q = 81) extend the range to q ≥ 81.
