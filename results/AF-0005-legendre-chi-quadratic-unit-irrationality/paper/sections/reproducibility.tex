\afbacksection{Reproducibility and formal verification}
\sloppy

\emph{Status of the result.} \Cref{thm:main} is conditional and is a preprint statement: it assumes four results of
the unrefereed preprint~\Kri{} (\cref{subsec:imported}), and no human expert independent of the AI systems that produced it
has reviewed the argument. Each step carries one of the labels of \cref{tab:status}. None of this is an independent
verification.

\emph{Computations.} Paths are relative to the directory of this contribution (AF-0005) in the AxiomForge archive
(\url{https://github.com/axiomforge-math/mathematics}); every command, its output and its outcome are recorded in
\path{logs/verification.md}. The scripts are in \path{logs/experiments/scripts/} and the step densities in
\path{logs/experiments/certificates/} (the directory \texttt{data/} of this manuscript):
\begin{itemize}
\item \path{real_certificates.py} (python-flint, Arb): the certificates of \cref{prop:certificates} and
\cref{app:certificates}, with the primal diagnostics at $n=10,25$;
\item \path{arithmetic_checks.py} (python-flint, exact rationals): the series identity, the entry bounds of
\cref{thm:trace-primes}, the regularisation and parity-minor checks of \cref{thm:prime-two}, the exact exponents
$n_p(h)$ (\cref{app:consistency});
\item \path{source_sets.py} (Arb): the exceptional sets of~\Kri{} and the list of new arguments;
\item the two step-density files \path{even-trace-comparison-densities.json} and \path{odd-trace-comparison-densities.json}.
\end{itemize}
They need Python~3.11 or later with \texttt{python-flint} and \texttt{mpmath}; none imports the code that produced the
candidate certificates. The archived exact verifiers of the candidate material were read and re-run in a scratch copy
and reproduce their recorded logs; they are not part of this package.

\emph{Formal verification.} The Lean~4 package in \path{lean/} (Lake package \texttt{axiomforge-af0005}, root module
\texttt{AxiomForge.AF0005}, namespace \texttt{AxiomForge.LegendreChi}, toolchain \texttt{leanprover/lean4:v4.34.0-rc1}, Mathlib
at revision \texttt{f385f9fd18abed332ebcd3df239cb357e264aceb}) contains: the definitions of $\chi_2$ and $\Ti_2$ with
their oddness; the quadratic units $z_\sigma(n)$ with $0<z<1$, $z^2+\sigma nz-\sigma=0$ and $1/z-\sigma z=n$, and the
identification of $T_\sigma(n)$ with $\chi_2(z)$ or $\Ti_2(z)$; the irrationality criterion (\cref{prop:criterion}) in
several forms; and the final deduction, conditionally: the theorems whose names end in \texttt{\_of\_estimates} derive the
irrationality at $n=14,27$ for both signs from a hypothesis \texttt{PencilEstimates} that packages (K1)--(K4) together with the
new estimates of \cref{thm:trace-primes,thm:prime-two}, at the rates of \cref{tab:certificates}; the margins are verified by
\texttt{norm\_num}. The Hankel polynomials $\Delta_h$ are not defined in the package, and neither the two new estimates nor
the shift lemma and monotonicity of \cref{prop:all-traces} are formalised, so these theorems are not formalisations of
\cref{thm:main}. The package builds with \texttt{lake exe cache get \&\& lake build}; \texttt{\#print axioms} reports only
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound} for each audited declaration (\path{lean/AXIOMS.txt}),
and the sources contain no \texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit}, \texttt{axiom} declaration or \texttt{native\_decide}.
No independent human review of this work has taken place.
