\section{The size certificates}\label{app:certificates}

\emph{Data.} Each certificate of \cref{prop:certificates} is a step density on $[0,2]$ with $N=160$ equal cells of
height $H_i/10^4$ ($H_i\in\Z$, $0\le H_i\le10^4$, $\sum_iH_i=80\cdot10^4$, so that the mass is exactly $1$) and
$\lambda=\Lambda_0/10^4$ with $\Lambda_0\in\Z$; the files \texttt{even-trace-comparison-densities.json} (keys $m,\sigma$ with
$n=2m$) and \texttt{odd-trace-comparison-densities.json} (keys $n,\sigma$) are in the directory \texttt{data/} of this
manuscript. Other keys of the files (traces $18,30$ and odd $21$ to $33$ and beyond) were used as cross-checks only.

\emph{Computation.} The script \path{real_certificates.py} (Arb ball arithmetic, 192 bits) does the following for each pair
and a mesh $1/L$, $L=4000$ on $[0,4]$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Exact integer checks of admissibility and alignment of the mesh; certified $z\le\sqrt5-2$.
\item $E(\mu)$ by the exact double antiderivative $g(u)=\frac{u^2\log\abs u}2-\frac{3u^2}4$ of $\log\abs u$ on each pair of
cells.
\item $F_\mu(x)=\sum_jc_jf(x-j/L)-tx$ with $f(u)=u\log\abs u-u$ (an antiderivative of $\log\abs u$), $t=t_\sigma(n)$ and explicit
coefficients $c_j$ (twice the differences of the heights, together with the terms $-\frac12f(x+1)+\frac12f(x-1)$ of $\psi$), exactly. Since
the mesh contains all points $j/L$ and $\pm1$, each term is on each mesh cell either convex or concave; on a cell every convex term is bounded above by its chord, every concave
term by its tangent at the midpoint, giving an affine majorant of $F_\mu-\lambda$; the integral of its positive part is
bounded above by the trapezoid rule (convexity of $(\cdot)_+$). Beyond $x=4$ nothing is integrated, see 5.
\item Hypothesis (i) for $x\ge4$. As $\operatorname{supp}\mu\subset[0,2]$ and $\int\mu=1$, $U^\mu(x)\le\log x$, and
$\psi(x)\ge\log(x-1)$ for $x\ge2$; so $F_\mu(x)\le2\log x-\log(x-1)-tx$ for $x\ge4$. The derivative of the right side is at most
$\frac12-t<-0.94$ and its value at $4$ is $2\log4-\log3-4t$, which is $\le\lambda-1.13$ (certified).
\item The integral over $[4,\infty)$ in \eqref{eq:Phi} vanishes: by 4, $F_\mu-\lambda<0$ there.
\item Hypothesis (ii): on $[-\frac1{100},0]$, $U^\mu\le\log2.01$, $\psi\ge\psi(0)=-1$ and $x\log z\le t/100$, so
$c_{\mathrm{cell}}=2\log2.01+1+\frac t{100}-\lambda$ is admissible (the values are in \cref{tab:certificates}).
\item $\Phi=-E+\lambda+\int$, the sum $\Car(n)+\Phi$, certified negative.
\end{enumerate}
The same script computes the primal value $J(\mu)$ of each density and the diagnostic quantity $\Car(n)+J\ge$ (a lower bound
for every dual value); it is positive at $n=10,25$.
