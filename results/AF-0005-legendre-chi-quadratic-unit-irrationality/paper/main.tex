\begin{abstract}
For an integer $n$ and a sign $\sigma$ let $z=2/(n+\sqrt{n^2+4\sigma})$, a unit of norm
$-\sigma$ of a real quadratic field, and let $T_\sigma(n)$ be the value at $z$ of Legendre's chi
function $\chi_2$ if $\sigma=+1$ and of the inverse tangent integral $\Ti_2$ if $\sigma=-1$.
Krisman proved, in an unrefereed preprint, that these numbers are irrational outside $47$ and
$57$ explicit values of $n$, and named two improvements of his local estimates that his data
suggested but that he did not prove. We prove both, without assumption: at an odd prime dividing $n$ the
denominators of his Hankel determinants have no cost beyond the one forced by the valuation of
$n$, and at the prime $2$, for odd $n$, the cost is half of the proved one. Assuming four results
of Krisman's preprint, which we state explicitly and do not reprove (his bound for the other odd
primes, his bound at $2$ for even $n$, used only for even $n$, his size estimate for even sizes $h\ge100$,
and his nonvanishing lemma, for primes $\ell\ge7$ with $\ell\nmid n$ and $\ell^2\nmid b$), and using
four new certificates for the size estimate, we deduce conditionally that $T_\sigma(n)$ is irrational for both
signs, every even $n\ge14$ and every odd $n\ge27$. This covers $31$ of the $47$ and $42$ of the
$57$ excluded values. The finite computations were redone by separate implementations. The work
has not been reviewed by an independent human expert and is not formally verified.
\end{abstract}
