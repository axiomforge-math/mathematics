## The traces n ≤ 25 (odd) and n = 10

* **Approach.** The same comparison with the new arithmetic constants at the smaller traces.
* **Obstruction.** The primal value of the stored density shows that no comparison density can close the comparison: C(n) + J is +0.0762 and +0.0867 at n = 10, +0.0128 and +0.0144 at n = 25 (σ = +1, −1).
* **Evidence.** Section 8 of the manuscript and `experiments/outputs/real-certificates.txt`.
* **Status.** Exact for this comparison with these constants; says nothing about the numbers themselves.
