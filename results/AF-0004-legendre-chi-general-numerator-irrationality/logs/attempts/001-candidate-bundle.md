## Goal

Prove irrationality of χ₂(a/q) and Ti₂(a/q) for the numerators a = 3, 4, 5, 8 and odd q beyond explicit thresholds.

## Method

The Hankel-determinant construction of the numerator-2 preprint with general numerator, with exact rational certificates.

## Result

A candidate proof with archived exact verifiers, rebuilt in this contribution with separately written checkers.

## Why it failed or what it taught

Nothing failed; the manuscript replaced it. The tail condition and the nonvanishing range were stated more simply (see `../CHANGELOG.md`).
