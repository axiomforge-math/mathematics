The four values lie below the ranges covered by the published and the recent preprint literature; Landen's identity only shows that each of the pairs {Li₂(2/11), Li₂(−2/9)} and {Li₂(2/9), Li₂(−2/7)} contains an irrational number. The proof uses normalised Hankel determinants of an affine moment functional attached to the weight D_{Bn}(x)³/D_{Cn}(x), of dimension Hn, and three estimates: a lower bound for the content (prime by prime, integrated with the prime number theorem), an upper bound for the value at the dilogarithm (Heine's formula and logarithmic energy, on the real axis or on two rays), and nonvanishing at prime indices (reduction to explicit local Hankel determinants).

## Reproducing

Requirements: Python 3.11+, TeX Live 2023+ with `latexmk`, poppler-utils, elan; for the
computations `python-flint` and `mpmath` (and SymPy where listed) at the versions of
`logs/experiments/requirements.txt`.

```bash
(cd paper && latexmk -pdf main.tex)                          # the manuscript
(cd lean && lake exe cache get && lake build)                 # the Lean package
python3 -m pip install -r logs/experiments/requirements.txt
(cd logs/experiments/scripts && python3 run_checks.py all)    # every computation
```

`run_checks.py` writes every output to `logs/experiments/outputs/` and exits with status 1
if a check fails; `logs/experiments/README.md` lists each program, what it establishes and its
run time. From the archive root, `python3 scripts/afm.py verify AF-0001` runs the archive checks.

## Known limitations and open points

* **No independent review.** All checks are AI-internal; the general arguments of Sections 3–8 have not been reviewed by a person, and the statement-fidelity review is pending.
* **The case 2/9 is delicate.** Its analytic bound is essentially optimal for its comparison measure (certified −1.34363 against −1.34391 recomputed on a fine mesh); the argument has about 0.019 of room on the arithmetic side and depends on Section 7 being exactly right. The exact data for n ≤ 11 neither confirm nor refute the bound (fits give limits between −1.349 and −1.341).
* **Error bounds inside the certificates** (weak duality and truncation of jet levels, bounded-variation midpoint rule, L¹ and uniform interpolation bounds, tail estimates) are proved in Appendix A but not independently reviewed.
* **Constants.** The O(h log h) terms of Sections 3, 7 and 8 and the rate of convergence in Lemma 5.4 are not explicit; a formal proof or an effective irrationality measure would need them.
* **Distribution argument.** Proposition 4.3 and Lemma 4.4 are tested only through their consequences on exact data.
* **Literature.** The method is that of Tang and Litsey with different parameters; the arguments were compared with Litsey's preprint at the level of statements only, the refereed thresholds are quoted second-hand, and no novelty is claimed for the general techniques (Section 1.2 of the manuscript).
* **Formalization.** Theorems 1.4–1.6 are not formalized; the Lean theorems are conditional on them.
