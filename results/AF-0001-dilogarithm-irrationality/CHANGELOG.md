# Changelog

All changes to this contribution are recorded here. Versions follow
`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` as defined in the archive's publication policy
(`docs/publication-policy.md`); each version is tagged `af-0001-vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`.
Versions `0.x` are preprints; `1.0.0` is reserved for the first publication at level V4.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added

- First preprint release: a complete written argument for the irrationality of
  Li₂(2/11), Li₂(2/9), Li₂(−2/9) and Li₂(−2/7) (`thm:main`), from three estimates for
  normalised Hankel determinants (content, archimedean decay, nonvanishing at prime
  indices), with the certificate programs, a separately written set of checkers, the exact
  computations for small `n` and a Lean development in preparation.

- Revision after a harsh AI-internal adversarial review of the manuscript (Claude Fable 5.2; see
  `logs/ai-contributions.md`): the review found no critical error, and all major findings were
  fixed (attributions to earlier work, the complete proof of Proposition 5.3, corrections of
  statements and of the description of the verification record).

### Corrected

These corrections were made while the argument was reconstructed from the original
candidate manuscripts, before this first release; they are recorded here for readers who
know the earlier versions.

- **Pole bound at ordinary primes.** As originally stated, the pole bound gave the pole
  `l = p` of the zero residue class the same weight as the other classes; it is then false
  by exactly one unit (for example for λ = 2/11, n = 6 at p = 19, 23, 29, 31). The pole
  `l = p` gains only `+1`, not `+2`. The bound is now stated with slots `w₀ + 1 + 2j` for
  the zero class (Proposition 4.6, Remark 4.7); it holds at every prime for every computed
  `n`, and the original form follows from it with a correction of `−2`. The jet bound and
  the asymptotic rates are unaffected.
- **Label of the 2/11 analytic certificate.** The number `0.602835` was described as an
  enclosure of the penalty integral `∫(W − θ)₊`; the integral is about `0.35243`, and
  `0.602835` is the certified upper bound that includes the error term of the midpoint
  rule (Appendix A.4).
- **Justification of the profile certificate.** The midpoint values of the arithmetic
  profile are now justified by convexity on each subinterval (Lemma A.1), with weak duality
  (Lemma B.5) as a second argument; the original text justified a different computation.
- **An unneeded and unproved claim removed.** A remark asserting, without proof, the
  p-adic convergence of finite geometric averages of non-polynomial functions was removed;
  the argument uses only an exact finite identity (Proposition 4.3).
- **Smaller statements.** The auxiliary quantity `Y_N` has coefficients in `p²ℤ_(p)` (not
  merely integral ones); the divisibility of alternating polynomials used in the pole bound
  is proved over `ℤ_(p)` (Lemma B.3); Remark 7.8 gives the exact change of scale between the
  2/11 certificate (`n²`, mass 4) and the `h²` normalisation.

### Verification

- The original certificate programs were re-run; all their checks pass.
- All certificates were recomputed by separately written checkers (exact rationals for the
  profile integrals, Arb ball arithmetic for transcendental quantities): every reported
  number and every strict inequality is confirmed; with exact profile integrals and a mesh
  of width 1/6000 the margins are 1.084 (n²-scale) for 2/11 and 0.019379, 0.13978, 0.02969
  per h² for 2/9, −2/9, −2/7.
- The eight local determinants were recomputed by two further programs; the Lean modules
  that prove them are regenerated byte for byte by the published generator.
- The exact polynomials `Q_n` were recomputed in the ranges of Appendix C, and the
  finite valuation bounds (including the corrected pole bound) checked at every prime.
- Lean: the package builds and the axiom audit of its 37 audited declarations reports only
  `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. The main theorem is proved in Lean only
  conditionally on the three estimates; their formalization is in preparation.
- All checks are AI-internal. No independent human review has taken place; the statement
  fidelity review is pending.
