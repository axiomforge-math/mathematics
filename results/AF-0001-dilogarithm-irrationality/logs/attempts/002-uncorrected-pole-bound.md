## Goal

Prove v_p(Q_n) ≥ Σ⁻_h of the slots w_σ + 2j + 2·[a_σ = 0, j = c_σ − a_σ − 1] for every class σ.

## Method

Comparison with exact Gauss valuations of the polynomials Q_n for n up to 11–19.

## Result

Refuted: deficits of exactly one unit at many (p, n).

## Why it failed or what it taught

The pole l = p of the zero class gains only +1 from p | l against −2 from τ_p. The corrected slots w₀ + 1 + 2j hold at every prime for every computed n.
