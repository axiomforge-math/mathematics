The curated history of <<AF-NNNN>>: how the result was found, what failed, what was
computed and how everything was verified. These files are written for readers. They
contain no internal identifiers, no references to unpublished material and no local
paths; every claim in them is either supported by a file of this contribution or marked
as unverified.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `timeline.md` | dated milestones from the first idea to publication |
| `insights.md` | the ideas that made the proof work, and why |
| `obstructions.md` | approaches that failed, and the precise reason |
| `attempts/NNN-<short-title>.md` | one record per substantial attempt (template: `attempts/000-template.md`) |
| `experiments/` | scripts, configurations, certificates and their outputs (see `experiments/README.md`) |
| `verification.md` | every check performed: commands, versions, outcomes, reviews |
| `ai-contributions.md` | the role of every AI system, and of the people involved (required) |

Status words follow the archive's verification policy: *proved*, *candidate*,
*heuristic*, *numerical evidence*, *refuted*. A log never upgrades the status of a
statement; `result.toml` and the manuscript are authoritative.
